    Privacy

    If you sign in, we store your email address, a display name if you choose one, and your learning plans: which seforim, your settings, and a record of each day you learned, missed or moved.

    We keep them only to show them back to you, on any phone or computer where you sign in. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone.

    They are kept with Google Firebase, the service that runs the sign-in and the saving. Each account can reach only its own plans.

    If you do not sign in, nothing leaves your phone: your plans are kept in this browser only.

    We may show counts of how many people learn each sefer, never who they are.

    We only show your name or what you are learning to other learners if you turn on "Let other learners see what I'm learning" in Settings. It is off unless you turn it on.

    You can delete your account and all your plans at any time: Settings, then Delete account.

    About

    Each day the app shows where to start and where to stop in your own printed sefer, named the way printed learning calendars name it: the amud, mishnah, pasuk, halacha, se'if or siman, and the first few words where the day stops. Days are sized by your settings, counting the commentaries you learn together with the text.

    Sources

    The sizes of the days and the few words that name each place come from texts published by Sefaria, using only Public Domain editions, except:

    The Gemara's text: Wikisource Talmud Bavli (תלמוד בבלי, ויקיטקסט), licensed CC BY-SA, from he.wikisource.org. The app keeps only the first few words of each place in it.
